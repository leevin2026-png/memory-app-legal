隐私政策

生效及更新：2026 年 7 月 30 日

简要说明：Memory 无需账号，不接入广告或第三方分析 SDK，开发者不会收集或接收你的照片、位置或使用数据。照片整理主要在设备本地完成。

1. 适用范围与负责人

本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的 Memory 应用（Bundle ID：com.story.mem.forever）。Memory 开发者负责本政策中描述的数据处理。隐私问题可发送至 leevin2026@gmail.com。

2. 我们不会收集的数据

Memory 不要求注册账户，不会把你的姓名、邮箱、照片、相册、精确位置或应用使用情况发送到开发者服务器。应用当前不集成第三方广告、用户分析或跨应用跟踪 SDK，也不会出售个人信息。

3. 设备上的照片处理

在你授权后，Memory 会在设备本地读取你允许访问的照片，以及照片自带的拍摄日期、媒体类型和位置等元数据，用于时间线、相册、色彩分类、回忆和足迹功能。你可以选择允许访问全部照片或仅部分照片。

添加到 Memory 相册、设置封面、收藏、复制、移动或删除照片等操作，只会在你主动执行并经过 iOS 授权后发生。照片原件仍由系统照片库管理。

4. 本地存储与缓存

应用设置、语言和主题偏好、Memory 内创建的相册与文件夹、收藏记录、封面选择、缩略图和图片缓存保存在你的设备上，用于提供功能和提升浏览速度。缓存不会替代照片原件，可在应用设置中清除；卸载应用会删除应用沙盒内的数据。

5. 位置、地图与 Apple 服务

Memory 不请求设备的实时定位。只有照片本身包含位置元数据时，足迹功能才会使用该信息。为了显示地图、城市名称和地点信息，相关坐标可能由 Apple 的 MapKit、地图或地理编码服务处理。该处理由 Apple 根据其政策提供，Memory 开发者不会接收这些坐标。你可以查阅 Apple 隐私政策。

6. 权限、保留与删除

  • 可在 iOS“设置”中将照片权限改为全部、有限访问或不允许。
  • 撤销权限后，依赖照片的浏览和整理功能将无法继续使用。
  • 可在 Memory 设置中清除图片缓存。
  • 可通过删除 Memory 内的相册或文件夹，移除相应的本地整理记录；这不会自动删除系统照片原件。
  • 卸载应用会移除应用本地数据；系统照片库中的照片仍按 iOS 的规则保留。

7. Apple 与 App Store 数据

当你下载应用、使用 App Store、iCloud 或 Apple 地图等 Apple 服务时，Apple 可能独立处理设备、购买、诊断或服务请求信息。这些处理由 Apple 决定和管理，不属于 Memory 开发者收集的数据。

8. 儿童隐私

Memory 不以收集儿童个人信息为目的，也不要求用户提供年龄或身份信息。由于开发者不收集账户、照片或位置数据，我们不会据此建立儿童用户档案。

9. 政策更新与联系

如果功能、第三方服务或适用要求发生变化，我们会更新本政策和页面日期，并在必要时于应用内说明。隐私或数据处理问题请联系：

leevin2026@gmail.com
ENGLISH VERSION

Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: July 30, 2026

Summary: Memory does not require an account, does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not send your photos, locations, or usage data to the developer. Photo organization is performed primarily on your device.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Memory app for iPhone and iPad (Bundle ID: com.story.mem.forever). The Memory developer is responsible for the processing described here. Contact leevin2026@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Data we do not collect

Memory does not require account registration and does not send your name, email address, photos, albums, precise location, or app usage to a developer-operated server. The current app contains no third-party advertising, user analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and the developer does not sell personal information.

3. On-device photo processing

After you grant permission, Memory locally reads the photos you allow it to access and associated metadata such as capture date, media type, and embedded location. This supports the timeline, albums, color organization, memories, and photo-footprint features. You may grant access to all or selected photos.

Actions such as organizing, choosing a cover, favoriting, copying, moving, or deleting photos occur only after an explicit action and the permissions required by iOS. Original photos remain managed by the system Photos library.

4. Local storage and cache

App settings, language and theme preferences, Memory albums and folders, favorites, cover choices, thumbnails, and image cache are stored on your device to provide features and improve browsing speed. Cache files do not replace original photos and can be cleared in Settings. Uninstalling Memory removes data stored in the app sandbox.

5. Location, maps, and Apple services

Memory does not request your device’s live location. Embedded photo location metadata is used only when available. To display maps, city names, and place information, coordinates may be processed by Apple MapKit, Maps, or geocoding services under Apple’s policies. The Memory developer does not receive those coordinates. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions, retention, and deletion

You may change Photos access to full, limited, or denied in iOS Settings. You can clear image cache in Memory, remove Memory albums and folders without automatically deleting original photos, or uninstall the app to remove app-local data. Photos in the system library remain subject to iOS Photos behavior.

7. Apple and App Store data

Apple may independently process device, purchase, diagnostic, or service-request information when you use the App Store, iCloud, Apple Maps, or other Apple services. Apple determines and controls that processing; it is not data collected by the Memory developer.

8. Children

Memory is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not ask users to provide age or identity information. The developer does not create child profiles from account, photo, or location data.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date if features, external services, or applicable requirements change, and provide in-app notice when appropriate.

leevin2026@gmail.com
